About Dr. Li

Biography

Fei-Fei Li is a Chinese woman who works in the computer science field, mostly in AI. She was born on July 3rd,1976 in Beijing, China. When she was 16, she immigrated to The United States. After high school, she attended Princeton University and got a bachelor's degree in physics. She would then go to Cal Tech and get a master's degree in electrical engineering. After that she bounced around different colleges and worked at them until she went back to Princeton and developed something called “ImageNet.” This application would revolutionize the way that people teach AI, due to this helping AI programs get a feel for the real world. This application would enhance what AIs could accomplish and make an AI program easier to train if given access to the database  

After Stanford, Dr. Li joined Google as a Google Cloud Vice president, with this job she promoted AI development and tried to make more efficient AI products. During her time at Google she would work on “Project Maven” which is something that the United States department of defense wanted Li and Google to make to intercept photos that were taken through drone cameras, they would do this via AI. Some people at Google did not like this project, so the department of security would need to ensure the Google workers know that it is not for “Offensive” purposes. Even with The Department of Security telling Li this she still states that “I do not want to weaponize AI” So she left Google in favor of going back to teaching at Stanford in 2018. Dr. Li continued to work at Stanford as a professor as well as working on projects outside of the school. Some of the things that she worked on were ImageNet which would be her star project. As well as something called World Labs that would create AI-generated worlds. Dr. Li has worked on a lot when it comes to the world of Artificial Intelligence and has made a huge impact in getting AI to the level that it is now, I strongly believe that her nickname, the “God Mother of AI”, really suits her!

Under Representation

Dr Li was underrepresented due to her being a Chinese woman and having to compete in a world dominated by males. Also, she moved from her home country, China, to the United States when she was just 16 years old. She faced a lot of bumps in the road on her journey to become the woman she is now. Growing up in the United States resulted in her having a language barrier with students and teachers. She also helped her family run a business, as well as having some wealth limitations. Despite all of these challenges she managed to be very capable in her career mainly in the AI realm, and even starting her own company, Image Net.

Contribution

Dr Li is a major contributor to AI research and creating AI, and some might say she is a pioneer of AI. Her impact in that field is very extensive. For example, she was one of the first to popularize AI. Not only that, but she also works with policy makers nationally, to enforce positive human-centered progress in AI based technology. Including several U.S senate and congressional testimonies. Additionally, she worked on spatial intelligence and ambient intelligence for health care delivery, and made her own company, the previously mentioned Image Net.

The future holds many possibilities for Li’s innovations. The new research in spacial intelligence will revolutionize how AI and AI powered robots interact with the physical world. It will give AI the tools to be able to understand the world in a more 3D way instead of just through images and pixels. With this technology robots could have the skills to fold laundry, sweep floors, and even eventually, be used in a more medical setting such as aiding in surgeries. Her contributions to technology will be imperative in the future of AI in our physical world.
